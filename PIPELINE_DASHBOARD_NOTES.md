# Research pipeline statistics

The dashboard includes all registered international journal manuscripts.

- Published: counted toward publication output and JCR/IF metrics.
- In revision: separately counted as active review pipeline.
- Submitted: separately counted as active review pipeline.
- Presented conference papers are excluded from journal pipeline.
- Annual published chart is retained. A separate annual activity chart shows all three statuses.
- Counts update from publications.json on page load.

If a manuscript is accepted, change its `status` to `Published` and verify its bibliographic details.
